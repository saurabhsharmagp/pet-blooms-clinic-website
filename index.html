About the Doctor

A decade of dedicated veterinary practice

With 10 years of clinical experience, Dr. Pooja Hirulkar (B.V.Sc & A.H.) has built a reputation for precise diagnosis and gentle, patient handling. Her practice centers on three demanding areas of veterinary medicine — skin disorders, organ health, and blood-related diseases — where careful diagnostics make all the difference for a pet's quality of life.

Dr. Pooja believes every pet deserves an accurate diagnosis and a treatment plan built around its individual needs — not a one-size-fits-all approach. Chronic conditions like kidney and liver disease, stubborn skin allergies, and blood disorders often require patience and continuity of care, and she works closely with pet parents at every step, explaining findings clearly and involving them in decisions about their companion's treatment.

Areas of Expertise

Focused specialties, thorough care

Dr. Pooja's practice is anchored in three demanding disciplines of small-animal medicine, each requiring precision, patience, and continuity of care.

Canine & Feline Dermatology

Diagnosis and management of skin allergies, infections, itching, hair loss, and chronic dermatological conditions in dogs and cats — tailored diet and treatment plans included.

Kidney & Liver Disease

Comprehensive evaluation and long-term management of renal and hepatic conditions, including diagnostics such as KFT, LFT and tailored nutritional support.

Blood-Related Diseases

Expert hands in identifying and managing hematological conditions, backed by detailed blood panel diagnostics including CBC, CRP, electrolytes and more.
